Why no resumes?

Resumes are self-reported — the applicant next to you can claim whatever they want, true or not. Skipping them means you're never competing against an exaggerated resume or a made-up credential.

It also closes a real risk: uploaded resumes can carry hidden prompt-injection instructions built to manipulate the AI systems screening candidates — letting manipulative applicants game the process without anyone noticing.

By working only from verified sources, Rundle makes sure the only thing that decides your match is what you can actually prove — not who told the best story.
